How to read this

Catchment circles show how far from the school the last child was offered a place in the normal admissions round (Reception for primaries, Year 7 for secondaries). London schools mostly have no fixed boundary, so the distance changes every year.

Faith schools rank baptised, practising families first, so their non-faith distance says little about your chances with a priest's reference.

Sold prices: HM Land Registry Price Paid Data, standard sales over the last two years in each area's postcode sectors. It does not record bedrooms, so flats (mostly 1–2 bed) and houses (mostly 2–4 bed) are shown separately. The price level compares each neighbourhood (an ONS Census LSOA, about 650 homes) with the shortlist median for the same property type, so 100 is typical and 150 is half as dear again.

Pupil numbers: DfE Get Information about Schools, SchoolDash and WhatSchool (Nov 2026). Rents and journey times are estimates. Rightmove links open the map view with 3+ bedrooms, houses only, garden, up to £4,500 a month. Ofsted: since Sept 2024 there are no new single-word grades; we show the last one and whether a later short visit kept it, or the new report card.
